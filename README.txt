DEEP SEA – LOST TREASURES
New browser-playable 3D prototype

DEPLOY TO NETLIFY
1. Keep this ZIP as your backup.
2. Extract the ZIP on your phone or computer.
3. In Netlify, open your site and go to Deploys.
4. Use the manual deploy / drag-and-drop area to upload the extracted folder contents.
5. Make sure index.html is at the top level of the uploaded folder.

IMPORTANT
- This prototype uses Three.js from jsDelivr, so the browser needs an internet connection.
- It is a playable prototype, not a finished AAA game.
- Touch controls: left joystick to swim, drag the right side of the screen to look, DIVE / RISE / COLLECT / BOAT buttons.
- Desktop controls: WASD or arrow keys; mouse drag to look; E to collect.
- Gameplay includes ship, diver, treasure collection, oxygen, health, shark attacks, selling treasure, equipment upgrades and location unlock progression.
- The existing live site is not changed until you manually deploy this new build.
